Global financial institution Goldman Sachs has recently issued a warning that the market's excessive expectations for AI investment need to be adjusted. This is interpreted as a signal calling for a cautious approach to the heightened atmosphere of optimism among investors, following the rapid growth of AI technology in recent weeks.

In particular, while acknowledging the potential of AI technology, Goldman Sachs pointed out the gap between the excessive expectations currently seen in the market and actual performance. This emphasizes that technological progress alone may not be enough to meet all expectations. There is a growing need for investors to comprehensively consider not only the technological achievements of AI companies but also the regulatory and ethical issues, as well as market entry barriers, that they face.

This warning is gaining even more attention in conjunction with the recent upward trend in AI-related stocks. Specifically, it contains a message cautioning that early investors are overly reliant on the future growth potential of AI companies. Goldman Sachs' position appears intended to induce investors to re-evaluate the practical value of the AI industry from a long-term perspective.

Market experts interpret this announcement as a call for a more balanced approach in future AI investment strategies. Instead of simply focusing on the innovative possibilities of AI technology, this requires a deeper understanding of how that technology can be applied and grow in the actual market.

In conclusion, Goldman Sachs' warning could serve as an opportunity to realistically adjust market expectations for AI investment. Investors will now need to pay attention not only to the technological progress of AI companies but also to the various challenges they face.
