Over the past 20 days, global markets have shown an unexpected reversal in direction. The shift from a previous hawkish policy stance to a bullish atmosphere is being interpreted as a signal of a market rally driven by expanding liquidity. This change has emerged alongside increased liquidity provision by central banks, empowering investors to increase their exposure to risky assets. In particular, the narrowing of credit spreads is sending positive signals in the corporate bond market. This trend suggests that, despite the continued existence of geopolitical uncertainties, market participants are placing greater expectations on economic recovery and growth potential. However, whether such a rally can be sustained will likely depend heavily on future policy decisions and trends in economic indicators.
