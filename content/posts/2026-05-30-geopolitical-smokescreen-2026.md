Over the past 12 days, a new perspective has emerged regarding the impact of geopolitical crisis narratives on markets and policy decisions. The shift from a previous trend of escalation to a neutral stance suggests that crisis narratives are not merely reflecting actual threats, but are being used as a smokescreen to manage market volatility and build policy justification. This approach can help regulate investor anxiety and justify policy actions as needed. However, if this smokescreen effect persists, market participants will likely recognize the gap between actual risks and policy responses, necessitating more cautious analysis and judgment.
