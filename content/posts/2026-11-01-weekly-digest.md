## Key Trends This Week
This week's global landscape was shaped by the complex intersection of monetary policy changes in financial markets and geopolitical tensions. The Fed's monetary policy direction intermittently shifted toward "hawkishness," signaling to financial markets, while China's economic growth provided hope for potential Asian economic cooperation but also heightened tensions due to international energy market and geopolitical factors. In the technology sector, moves toward strengthening AI and cryptocurrency regulations are driving strategic changes in financial institutions.

## [Monetary Policy and Financial Market Trends]
The Fed's (FOMC) monetary policy provided uncertainty to financial markets as it shifted from neutral to intermittent hawkishness. Movements in 10-year Treasury yields, credit spreads, and the dollar index suggest the possibility of coordination among global financial institutions. In particular, recent policy changes are strengthening links with international financial institutions (BIS), requiring economic forecasts and strategic adjustments by investors.

## [Geopolitical Trends]
China's Q3 GDP growth exceeded expectations, demonstrating economic resilience and raising possibilities for Asian economic cooperation. However, this simultaneously acted as a factor heightening geopolitical tensions. Specifically, geopolitical issues related to Iran and energy market volatility following the US midterm election results are impacting international supply chains and crude oil prices. In this process, the roles of the UK government and the Trump administration are also drawing attention.

## [Asset Transition and Tech Trends]
Positive changes appeared in asset allocation and technology sectors. Strategic adjustments by Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan sent positive signals to the KOSPI and ETF markets, and VanEck's new Wealth Builder ETF is accelerating the integration of various asset classes, including cryptocurrencies. Furthermore, the movement toward stricter AI regulation reflects the process of financial institutions seeking a balance between technological advancement and oversight.

## [Other Trends]
- As the movement to strengthen cryptocurrency regulation accelerates, particularly following the Binance hacking incident, the intensification of FIU regulations is expected to impact the virtual asset market.
- Major financial institutions are showing resistance toward tokenized assets, implying structural changes in the financial markets.

## Weekly Conclusion: Looking Ahead to Next Week
The trends of this week are expected to continue into next week. Financial markets will remain focused on changes in the Fed's monetary policy and the subsequent reactions of global financial institutions, while geopolitical tensions will influence energy prices and the potential for international cooperation. In the technology sector, changes in AI and cryptocurrency regulations are expected to further complicate the strategic directions of financial institutions. As these factors interact complexly, the global economy appears to need preparation for various scenarios.
