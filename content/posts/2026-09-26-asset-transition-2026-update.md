The atmosphere of the financial markets, which had been progressing under an 'escalation' stance until 20 days ago, has recently shifted to 'bullish,' with rising Treasury yields demonstrating new asset allocation patterns. This change reflects the market's reaction to the Fed's monetary policy adjustments and inflation management.

The rise in Treasury yields indicates that investors are preparing for inflation and the possibility of interest rate hikes. However, even within this environment, the technology sector is showing strength, while cyclical industries tend to be relatively retreating. This signifies that the market maintains confidence in future growth drivers while simultaneously expressing caution toward short-term risk factors.

These asset transition trends suggest that investors are pursuing stable yields while continuing to invest in sectors with high innovation and growth potential. The complex interaction between changes in Fed policy and market reactions provides important implications for the future economic outlook and asset allocation strategies.
