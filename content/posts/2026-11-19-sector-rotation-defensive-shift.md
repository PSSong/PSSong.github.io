In recent market trends over the past 26 days, the rapid shift from a neutral stance to a bearish one clearly demonstrates investors' risk-averse tendencies. Specifically, the strength in the utilities and consumer staples sectors distinctly reflects this change. Along with the rise in the VIX index, investors are moving funds into defensive assets that provide stable returns.

While the previous neutral market sentiment reflected efforts to maintain balance amidst uncertainty, the recent bearish shift suggests that investors are seeking safer havens as economic uncertainty and geopolitical risks increase. The strength in consumer staples and utilities is interpreted as a direct result of this risk-aversion tendency. These sectors tend to guarantee relatively stable returns even during economic downturns, clearly illustrating investors' pursuit of stability.

This shift goes beyond a simple reallocation of assets; it suggests that market participants are becoming more cautious regarding future economic outlooks. Therefore, interest in defensive assets is expected to persist, which is likely to make the overall market sentiment even more conservative.
