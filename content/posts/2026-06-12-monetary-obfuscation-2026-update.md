The possibility of constraints on the Fed's independence is introducing new uncertainties into the financial markets. The recent strength in the dollar index and the widening of high-yield (HY) credit spreads reflect investors' risk-aversion tendencies, implying concerns over potential changes in economic policy. However, amidst these trends, the stabilization of the volatility index (VIX) and the relative stability of Bitcoin (BTC) prices are making market interpretations even more complex.

Over the past 26 days, the Fed's stance has shifted from 'hawkish' to 'neutral.' This shift goes beyond a simple adjustment in policy direction; it suggests a change in the relationship between the government and the central bank. The possibility of constraints on the Fed's independence implies the intervention of political factors in the policy-making process, which could weaken predictability and stability in the financial markets. Nevertheless, the stabilization of the VIX index suggests that market participants are absorbing these changes to some extent. The price stability of Bitcoin also demonstrates the independent momentum of the digital asset market, suggesting the possibility of new investment flows even amidst uncertainty in traditional financial markets.

These complex signals emphasize that investors must be even more cautious in managing market uncertainty. The possibility of constraints on the Fed's independence and the subsequent reactions in the financial markets remain key elements that must be continuously monitored.
