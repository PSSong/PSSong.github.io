Recent financial markets are filled with complex signals. While high-yield (HY) credit spreads are narrowing, market participants appear to be increasing their preference for risky assets. However, in contrast to this risk-on momentum, the rise in the VIX index and the decline in US 10-year Treasury yields continue to reveal uncertainty within the market.

This shift in trend, which has continued since 30 days ago, reflects a transition from 'hawkish' to 'neutral.' As the previous hawkish monetary policy stance eases, the market is experiencing temporary stability. However, this does not signify a complete pivot toward risk-on; rather, it represents cautious optimism amidst uncertainty.

In particular, the rotation into defensive sectors suggests that market participants are still preparing for future uncertainties. This indicates that while economic conditions are improving, vigilance against potential risk factors remains. Therefore, the narrowing of credit spreads and changes in sector rotation can be interpreted as mixed signals reflecting the complex psychological state of the market.
