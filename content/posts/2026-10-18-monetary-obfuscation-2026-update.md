October 18, 2026 - Recently, financial markets have been exhibiting confusion amid uncertainty regarding the Fed's policy direction. In particular, investor risk aversion is intensifying as credit spreads on high-yield bonds rise and the VIX index increases. However, at the same time, defensive sectors are maintaining their strength due to an increase in net liquidity.

The recent shift in the Fed's policy direction from 'neutral' to a 'hawkish stance' is a noteworthy change. This shift carries significance beyond simply increasing the possibility of interest rate hikes. In the process of seeking a balance between economic recovery and inflation management, the Fed is playing a role in amplifying uncertainty by failing to send clear signals to the market. This ambiguity makes it difficult for investors to predict future economic conditions, consequently leading to an increase in credit spreads and volatility indices.

However, the Fed's liquidity supply policy continues to have a positive impact on defensive sectors. This can be interpreted as part of the Fed's efforts to maintain economic stability. Consequently, even amidst uncertainty, the market is showing a tendency to seek safe havens, which is why defensive sectors such as technology and healthcare are maintaining their strength.

This situation serves as an example of the complex impact that Fed policy decisions have on the market, highlighting the need for investors to read between the lines of policy and prepare for various scenarios. It is a time to closely monitor the Fed's next move and the subsequent market reaction.
