Over the past week, financial markets have witnessed a new pattern: the expanding participation of major financial institutions in on-chain finance. Previously, these movements were noted under a positive, 'bullish' outlook; however, the recent situation has shifted toward 'escalation,' heightening tension. This change can be interpreted as more than mere participation; it is a preparatory stage for a power shift from the traditional financial system toward tokenized assets and Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs).

At the root of this transition lies structural changes in financial markets alongside accelerated digitalization. The growing interest of financial institutions in tokenized assets using blockchain technology signals a goal that goes beyond the mere digitalization of existing assets, aiming instead to build a more efficient and transparent financial system. In particular, the introduction of CBDCs is highly likely to serve as a means to improve the efficiency of cross-border financial transactions while simultaneously strengthening the control of central banks.

However, the recent shift in direction suggests that this process is progressing more rapidly than expected. This is amplifying anxiety among market participants, meaning that finding a balance between trust in the existing financial system and adaptation to the new digital financial structure has become even more critical. These changes have the potential to reshape the future of financial markets, and it is a point in time where both investors and policymakers are required to adopt new strategic approaches.

Ultimately, the participation of major financial institutions in on-chain finance heralds a fundamental change in the power structure, moving beyond a simple technological transition. The speed and scale of this change provide important hints as to who will take the lead in the future financial ecosystem.
