On June 4, global financial markets are experiencing a state of confusion due to discrepancies in interest rate and liquidity-related data. Recent economic indicators are showing different directions, sending complex signals to market participants.

The narrowing of HY credit spreads and the strength of tech stocks suggest that investors are increasing their preference for risky assets. This phenomenon reflects positive outlooks regarding economic recovery and corporate financial health. In contrast, however, the rise in the volatility index (VIX) and the sharp decline in Bitcoin (BTC) strongly indicate instability within the market.

Notably, this discrepancy coincides with the shift in monetary policy direction that began on May 13, 2026. Central banks, which had maintained a 'hawkish' stance, have recently been showing a transition toward a 'neutral' stance. This change reflects the confusion arising from the process of readjusting market expectations.

Behind this transition lies the complex interaction of the global economy. It is a result of central banks' efforts to balance inflationary pressures with economic growth. However, the impact of this policy shift on the market is inconsistent. On one hand, expectations for liquidity expansion are rising, but on the other hand, anxiety due to increasing uncertainty is being amplified.

Through these discrepancies, investors have once again confirmed the complexity of the market. Along with the movement of the Dollar Index, the sharp volatility of BTC signals that uncertainty remains high even in the digital asset market. This emphasizes that investors must comprehensively analyze and judge various indicators.

Ultimately, this market confusion goes beyond a simple discrepancy between economic indicators, reflecting uncertainty regarding the future outlook of the global economy. The gap between central bank policy changes and market reactions remains a crucial indicator that must be continuously observed.
