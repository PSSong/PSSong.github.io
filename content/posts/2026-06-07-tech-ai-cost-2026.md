JPMorgan's recent warnings regarding AI-related costs are raising new concerns about the future of the tech industry. AI technology, which was previously regarded as a growth driver amidst bullish outlooks, is now emerging as a potential RISK-OFF factor. This shift reflects more than just rising technology costs; it reflects the structural challenges and uncertainties regarding future profitability faced by tech companies.

Over the past few weeks, particularly following the trend that began 14 days ago, investor attitudes toward the tech sector are fundamentally changing. JPMorgan's warning emphasizes that the high cost burden of AI development and operation could pressure corporate profits. This is also linked to Tesla's revaluation, demonstrating the impact that increasing costs in the electric vehicle and autonomous driving technology sectors have on market expectations.

These changes in the tech industry have the potential to impact global economic trends beyond the issues of individual companies. Investors are now re-evaluating not only the innovative potential of AI technology but also the accompanying financial burdens and risks. This transition calls for a more cautious approach to the future growth prospects of the tech sector, and the market's shift in direction provides important implications for future investment strategies.
