This movement can be interpreted as a strategy responding to the contemporary trend where the importance of digital financial services is being further emphasized.

Such innovation has the potential to provide customers with more choices and trigger structural changes in the financial market. Therefore, beyond a simple interest rate adjustment, this appears to be an important signal suggesting a future-oriented direction for the development of the financial industry.
