Uncertainty regarding the Fed's recent policy direction is sending complex signals to financial markets. As the Fed, which had maintained a neutral stance until just 29 days ago, suddenly shifted toward a hawkish direction, the market is showing confused reactions. While this change has strengthened expectations for inflation management and potential interest rate hikes, it has simultaneously amplified doubts among market participants regarding the lack of policy transparency.

The rise in the dollar index and the widening of credit spreads reflect a risk-averse atmosphere. These indicators suggest that investors prefer moving toward safe-haven assets amidst uncertainty. However, trends in the VIX index and Bitcoin (BTC) show that some risk-taking is still occurring within the market. The relative stability of the VIX index and the volatility of BTC imply that investors are still exploring various levels of risk.

This discrepancy clearly demonstrates the complex impact that Fed policy decisions have on the market. In a situation where clear signals regarding the Fed's future moves are lacking, investors are adjusting their strategies by considering various scenarios.
