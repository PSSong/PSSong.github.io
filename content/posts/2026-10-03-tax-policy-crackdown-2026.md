The U.S. Treasury and the IRS are recently strengthening their crackdown on capital gains tax avoidance strategies employed by the wealthy. In particular, intensive monitoring of tax avoidance strategies utilizing ETFs (Exchange Traded Funds) is underway, a move that is being interpreted as emphasizing the role of tax policy as a means of social control, moving beyond simple tax collection issues.

As recently as six days ago, the IRS's stance was relatively 'bullish.' That is, it maintained a tone of encouraging market growth and investment activities. However, a recent shift in direction has occurred toward a 'hawkish' attitude. This change aligns with the government's objectives of addressing economic inequality and strengthening fiscal health. Specifically, as tax avoidance strategies by the wealthy are identified as factors deepening economic imbalance, the intensified IRS crackdown reflects the determination to resolve these issues.

This intensification of enforcement appears to be part of an effort to secure social justice and economic fairness, going beyond mere tax collection. At the same time, however, uncertainty and vigilance are increasing among investors. Interest is growing regarding the impact these policy changes will have on financial markets and the response strategies for investors in the future.
