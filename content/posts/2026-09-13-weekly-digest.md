## Key Flows This Week
This week's global situation showed a pattern where the continuous escalation of geopolitical manipulation coexisted with relative stability in financial markets. Conversely, global financial markets maintained a stable flow as central banks kept a neutral stance. In terms of technological innovation, policy changes regarding AI and blockchain technology are progressing in a positive direction, and asset markets are showing some changes driven by strength in the energy and healthcare sectors.

The most prominent trend this week was the escalation of geopolitical tensions between BRICS nations and Western nations. This situation shows a tendency to continuously rise, suggesting that tensions on the international political stage are continuing to increase.
