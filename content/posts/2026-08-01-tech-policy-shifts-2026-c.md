Until now, this project has reflected a positive outlook ('bullish') in the financial market, showing an optimistic attitude toward the introduction of innovative technologies; however, the shift in direction over the past 20 days suggests new challenges along with the complexity of the situation. The change toward 'escalation' implies the need to consider regulatory and economic variables that may arise alongside technological progress.

This experiment suggests the possibility of introducing Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) alongside improvements in the efficiency of the international payment system. Such changes could reshape existing financial structures and promote innovation in cross-border payment systems. However, these innovations simultaneously demand a review of the international financial regulatory framework and highlight the need for international cooperation and regulatory harmonization.

Technical difficulties and legal constraints that may arise during the project's progress will pose new challenges to financial market participants. Therefore, it is crucial to continuously monitor the progress of the Agorá project and find a balance between technological advancement and regulatory change. This will be a key element in understanding the future of the international financial system.

In conclusion, the BIS Agorá project heralds fundamental changes in the international financial structure, necessitating continuous attention to the ripple effects these changes will bring and the corresponding adaptation strategies.
