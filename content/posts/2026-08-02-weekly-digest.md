## Key Trends This Week
This week demonstrated significant changes at the intersection of global finance and technology. As discussions regarding the introduction of Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) continue, structural changes in the financial system are anticipated, with prominent movements from companies seeking new growth engines by blurring the boundaries between technology and finance. Meanwhile, geopolitical tensions continue to escalate, and asset markets are exhibiting a risk-aversion tendency, indicating a shift toward defensive assets.

## [Changes in CBDCs and Monetary Policy]
**Changes in Central Bank Digital Currencies and Monetary Policy**  
Discussions on the introduction of CBDCs imply the potential for changes in the global status of the US dollar, and the strategic movements of central banks are accelerating. With the successful progress of the BIS's Project Agora, positive outlooks regarding the adoption of CBDCs are spreading. In particular, the collaboration between JP Morgan and BlackRock demonstrates an attempt to create new business models by breaking down the barriers between the financial and technology sectors. Their trends have shifted from an initially neutral stance to a positive outlook, followed by a period of heightened tension that has since eased. These changes suggest the possibility of a realignment in the global financial system.

## [Escalating Geopolitical Tensions]
**Escalation of Geopolitical Tensions**  
On the geopolitical front, tensions between Russia and Western nations are steadily rising. As the roles of international organizations, including the IMF and BIS, and financial elites are emphasized, political instability in various regions, such as Iran and Argentina, is impacting international financial markets. This escalation of tension is increasing uncertainty in the financial markets.

## [Asset Shift Trends]
**Defensive Shift in Asset Markets**  
The asset market is showing a shift toward defensive strategies, characterized by strength in the energy and healthcare sectors and weakness in the technology and consumer goods sectors. While the trend was initially positive, the market sentiment has recently reversed to weakness due to risk-aversion tendencies. This trend suggests that investors are reallocating funds into more stable assets.

## [Other Trends]
- **Cryptocurrency Regulation**: The cryptocurrency market is being negatively impacted by strengthened regulations and changes in investor sentiment, with regulatory pressure intensifying recently.
- **Financial Reorganization**: Changes in central bank policies are progressing gradually, and the strategic relocation of companies, such as Jushi Holdings' headquarters move, reflects movements seeking tax benefits and regulatory advantages.

## Weekly Conclusion: Looking Ahead to Next Week
This week's trends suggest structural changes in the global financial system and the ongoing escalation of geopolitical tensions. The progress of CBDC introduction and the convergence of technology and finance have the potential to form a new paradigm in financial markets, while the defensive shift in asset markets reflects investors' response to uncertainty. While geopolitical tensions are expected to continue, the policy adjustments of central banks and the strategic relocations of corporations can be interpreted as efforts toward market stabilization. It is necessary to watch how these trends interact and evolve in the coming week.
