Over the past 16 days, a noticeable increase in applications for financial innovation services and the recruitment of companies for the TechFinder Showcase has further highlighted the close connection between the development of the fintech industry and technological innovation networks. While this movement initially took on a positive and optimistic tone, the recent shift toward 'escalation' suggests that greater involvement and strategic adjustments are required.

At the root of this change lies the recognition that the impact of technological innovation on the financial industry is steadily growing. In particular, the participation of agencies such as DARPA can be interpreted as a reflection of the will to expand the scope of technological innovation and rebalance the equilibrium between existing financial regulations and innovation. This trend suggests that beyond simply promoting technological advancement, the integration and management of technology networks at a national level has become increasingly important.

The shift in policy direction does not merely signify increased regulation; rather, it reflects the process of finding a complex balance between innovation and safety. Companies are now faced with greater regulation alongside more opportunities for collaboration, which could serve as an opportunity to further solidify their positions within the global technological innovation ecosystem. At the same time, however, these changes will demand more systematic strategy formulation and response capabilities from companies.

Such policy changes herald structural changes in technology networks alongside the growth of the fintech industry. Amidst these trends, companies must thoroughly prepare to adapt to the changing regulatory environment while capturing new opportunities.
