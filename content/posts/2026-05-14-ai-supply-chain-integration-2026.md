Recent issues have shifted from a trend of escalating tensions toward a more neutral direction, reflecting the reality where technological cooperation and competition between the two nations are intricately intertwined.

Until six days ago, the technological competition between the two nations was on an intensifying trend, but recently, the situation has shown a tendency toward stabilization. This change can be interpreted as a result of efforts by global companies such as Nvidia and Cisco to maintain continuous activities in both markets while minimizing political risks.

Behind this transition lies the fact that both nations recognize the importance of technological innovation while remaining wary of the negative impact that excessive confrontation could have on the global supply chain. In particular, cooperation and competition in the field of AI provide advantages to both nations while simultaneously playing a role in strengthening mutual interdependence.

This trend toward neutralization demonstrates that the technological competition between the US and China is not merely hostile, but reflects a complex mechanism of maximizing mutual benefits while simultaneously managing tensions. Moving forward, the relationship between the two nations is expected to continue evolving in accordance with international technological trends and economic conditions, all while attempting to maintain this balance.
