Recently, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and the blockchain-based financial platform Circle have been strengthening their networks and actively participating in Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) and digital asset projects. The movements of these institutions signal a fundamental change in the financial system that goes beyond simple cooperation. The shift from a previous "de-escalation" stance to "escalation" demonstrates that the commitment of these institutions toward financial technology innovation has become even stronger.

This strengthening of networks is expected to accelerate the digitalization of financial services alongside changes in the regulatory environment for digital assets. In particular, participation in CBDC projects will promote cooperation between central banks and private financial institutions, likely reshaping future financial infrastructure into a more interconnected form. These changes are expected to contribute not only to improving the efficiency of financial transactions but also to enhancing the stability and accessibility of the global financial system.

However, as such innovations may simultaneously raise regulatory and security issues, financial institutions must focus on continuous regulatory compliance and security enhancement alongside technological advancements.
