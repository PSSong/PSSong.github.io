The rapid advancement and adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology are bringing fundamental changes to the job structure of Wall Street. Until just 17 days ago, this issue maintained a neutral stance, as uncertainty existed regarding the changes AI's introduction would bring. However, as the direction has recently shifted from 'neutral' to 'bullish,' expectations are rising that AI will bring positive innovation to the financial industry.

Behind this change lies the increased efficiency and predictive capabilities regarding complex economic environments provided by AI. AI is complementing or replacing human roles in various fields such as risk management, investment analysis, and customer service, thereby restructuring existing job frameworks. At the same time, new roles requiring new technical expertise and data analysis capabilities are being created.

Financial experts predict that through these changes, the financial industry will evolve in a more sophisticated and efficient direction. In the future, innovative services and solutions utilizing AI technology are expected to lead the market, making continuous learning and technological adaptation essential for those working in the financial sector.
