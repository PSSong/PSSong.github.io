Market trends that have been ongoing since 20 days ago are recently showing a shift in direction from 'de-escalation' to 'bearish,' making the decline in the value of gold and Bitcoin even more prominent. This change goes beyond simple fluctuations in asset values, reflecting changes in investor psychology and reactions to economic uncertainty.

While gold and Bitcoin have traditionally been considered safe-haven assets, the current decline is being triggered by two factors. First, as global economic uncertainty increases, investors' tendency to avoid risk is strengthening. Second, as concerns about inflation persist, investors have come to place greater importance on liquidity and stability. These complex factors have weakened the demand for gold and Bitcoin, which had been performing their roles as safe-haven assets.

These trends suggest that the market is seeking a balance between the uncertainty of economic recovery and inflationary pressures. Future market trends are expected to react even more sensitively to changes in central bank monetary policies and economic indicators. Therefore, investors will need to employ strategies that consider the balance between inflation and economic growth alongside risk management.
