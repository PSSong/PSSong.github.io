The Fed's recent interest rate decision is sending complex signals to the market. As the Fed, which had maintained a neutral stance until just 30 days ago, suddenly shifted toward a more hawkish stance, financial markets are showing confused reactions. While this shift is interpreted as reflecting a strong commitment to curbing inflation, market interpretations vary.

The narrowing of HY credit spreads and the decline in the VIX (fear index) show a tendency for investors to take on more risk. However, the rise in US 10-year Treasury yields and increased volatility in the dollar index suggest that uncertainty remains. The impact of these Fed decisions on the market is two-sided: on one hand, it is being received as a positive signal for economic recovery, but on the other hand, voices expressing concern over additional financial instability are growing louder.

The Fed's recent actions reflect a complex judgment regarding the economic situation. It appears to be attempting to strike a balance between efforts to control inflation and maintaining the sustainability of economic growth. These mixed signals are making it difficult for investors to predict future outlooks, further heightening interest in the Fed's next move.
