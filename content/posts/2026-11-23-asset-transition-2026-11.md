JPMorgan's recent report presents a new direction for capital flows in the financial markets through its positive outlook on the tech and gaming industries. This shift goes beyond a mere emotional change in the market; it reflects deep confidence in corporate technology investments and the continuous growth potential of the gaming industry.

Behind this change lies the acceleration of digital transformation alongside the global expansion of the gaming industry. Even amidst ongoing economic uncertainty, the inflow of capital into these sectors suggests that investors are searching for future growth drivers.

However, despite these positive outlooks, the market remains exposed to various risk factors.
