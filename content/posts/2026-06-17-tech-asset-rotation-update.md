Over the past 10 days, the tech sector has maintained a positive trend alongside the remarkable growth of SpaceX. The increase in SpaceX's valuation goes beyond the success of a single company; it has served as an opportunity to reaffirm investor confidence in space technology and innovation. This trend has created a positive ripple effect across the tech sector, leading to the revaluation of other technology companies.

However, even amidst this positive atmosphere, market uncertainty persists. The ongoing volatility in the VIX, or the volatility index, suggests that investors remain wary of future uncertainties. In particular, the price volatility of Bitcoin (BTC), a leading representative of the cryptocurrency market, reflects global economic instability and is unsettling investor sentiment.

Such phenomena make asset rotation within the tech sector even more complex. While the success of companies like SpaceX sends positive signals to the market, the trends in the VIX and BTC serve as a reminder that investors must approach with caution. Therefore, the future of the tech sector appears to depend heavily on the stability of investor sentiment alongside the rise in corporate values.

This is a phenomenon that reflects the complexity of the global economy, extending beyond just the tech sector. Investors must closely observe these interactions and seek a balance between corporate value and market sentiment.
