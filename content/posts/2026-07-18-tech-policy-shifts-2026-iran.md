Furthermore, this situation reveals the complex interaction between technological regulation and security policy. There is a growing need for governments and corporations to approach more cautiously how military tensions affect technology policy. This goes beyond mere economic loss, posing fundamental questions about the future of technological innovation and global cooperation.
