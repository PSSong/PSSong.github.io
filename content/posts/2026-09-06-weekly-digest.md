## Key Flows This Week
This week's global situation showed a strengthening preference for risk assets within a complex environment where central bank monetary policies and shifts in geopolitical alliances intersect. However, despite these positive movements, changes in central bank policies and geopolitical tensions continue to maintain market volatility. In particular, the strengthening cooperation between India and Russia and attacks on Ukrainian energy facilities are impacting regional alliance structures and energy security.

## [Financial Policy and Market Trends]
**Central Bank Policies and Financial Market Stability**
Discussions regarding central bank monetary policies continued but generally maintained a neutral direction. Policy trends from the FSS and the Fed did not bring significant changes to financial markets, and despite fluctuations in credit spreads and the dollar index, the market is maintaining stability without major turmoil. However, volatility in the VIX still reflects market uncertainty.

## [Asset Rotation and Technology Investment]
**Rise in Technology Assets and Risk Appetite**
The rise in gold and Bitcoin prices reflects a risk-on market sentiment. Global investors, centered around BlackRock and JPMorgan, are expanding investments in risk assets, leading to increased interest in technological infrastructure and digital assets. In particular, BlackRock's expansion of technology and AI investment has the potential to bring changes to the global regulatory environment. These trends demonstrate investors' will to move away from the uncertainty that has persisted over the past few-weeks.

## [Geopolitical Trends]
**Changes in Geopolitical Alliance Structures**
The strengthening cooperation between India and Russia, coupled with efforts to resolve the war between Ukraine and Russia, is raising possibilities of changes in the alliance structure of the Indo-Pacific region. These trends show a situation where geopolitical tensions continue alongside China's Belt and Road Initiative. Alliances involving the US and EU also appear to be readjusting amidst these changes.

## [Fintech and Digital Assets]
**Reshaping of the Digital Asset Market**
Plans by Goldman Sachs and Bank of America to launch stablecoins are accelerating the pace at which traditional banks enter the digital asset market. This is reshaping the competitive landscape in the fintech sector and demanding changes in risk management strategies.

### Other Trends
- **IMF's Cryptocurrency Policy Changes**: The IMF is showing a shift in its national capital flow strategy through movements to support private donations for Bitcoin growth in El Salvador.
- **AI Economy and Demographics**: Goldman Sachs presented a negative outlook regarding South Korea's aging population and the growth potential of the AI economy.
- **Energy Geopolitics**: Attacks by Ukraine on Russian energy facilities are posing a significant threat to Russia's energy security.

## Weekly Conclusion: Looking Ahead to Next Week
This week's flow shows that while the global economy is increasing investment in risk assets, it still carries uncertainty due to central bank policies and geopolitical tensions. Next week, it is necessary to watch how the strengthening cooperation between India and Russia will affect international alliance structures and what ripple effects the strategic changes of companies in the technology and digital asset sectors will bring to the market. These trends are highly likely to act as potential variables in the process of global economic stabilization.
