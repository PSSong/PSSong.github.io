This transition can be interpreted as more than just an improvement in relations; it is a signal that both nations are seeking a new trade management system. Recent diplomatic movements demonstrate the will of both nations to re-evaluate existing conflict structures and seek a new framework that can maximize mutual interests.

Specifically, the keywords "mutual respect" and "mutual cooperation" mentioned in high-level talks between the two nations present a new paradigm of mutual understanding and cooperation, moving away from previous confrontational language. This change is highly likely to serve as a significant turning point that goes beyond mere trade issues to readjust the balance of the global economy.

However, whether these changes will lead to substantive policy changes remains uncertain. Considering the complex interests and the ongoing competitive structure between the two nations, future movements require even closer observation. The international community is watching closely to see how these changes in diplomatic nuance will affect the future trade order and geopolitical balance.

At the background of this transition lies the necessity for continuous dialogue and mutual understanding between the U.S. and China. This may be a signal that demands a fundamental re-evaluation of the global economic and security structures, going beyond a mere bilateral issue. Therefore, future diplomatic movements and policy changes are expected to play a crucial role in shaping a new framework for the international trade order.
