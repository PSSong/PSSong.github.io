As political pressure regarding the Fed's independence intensifies, actual market trends appear to be maintaining liquidity dominance. While recent controversies are amplifying concerns that the Fed may not be entirely free from political influence, the financial markets are continuing a stable trend through liquidity expansion and narrowing credit spreads.

Over the past few weeks, the Fed has demonstrated efforts to maintain the independence of its monetary policy despite political pressure. However, as this pressure persists, market participants are raising observations that the Fed's policy decisions could be influenced by external factors. Nevertheless, liquidity indicators remain strong, suggesting that investors are still maintaining their preference for risky assets.

In particular, the narrowing of credit spreads reflects a recovery of confidence in the corporate bond market, suggesting the possibility of a sustained economic recovery. This trend implies that the Fed's policy direction is still exerting a positive influence on the market.

However, the recent shift in sentiment is noteworthy. While a bullish stance previously prevailed, market outlooks are changing as the Fed's stance has recently turned hawkish. This suggests the possibility that the Fed may focus more heavily on inflation management despite political pressure, implying increasing uncertainty regarding the future direction of monetary policy. These changes are prompting investors to observe liquidity and credit market trends more closely.
