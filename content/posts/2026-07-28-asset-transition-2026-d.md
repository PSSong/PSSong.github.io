As market trends have shifted from 'neutral' to 'bullish' over the past 16 days, the strength of the energy sector and the upward trend in the healthcare sector are drawing significant attention. This shift is interpreted as part of a defensive asset rotation, where investors seek stable returns amidst economic uncertainty. The energy sector is gaining momentum driven by geopolitical factors and rising commodity prices, while the healthcare sector is gaining investor confidence based on its long-term growth potential and stable profitability.

In contrast, the technology and consumer discretionary sectors are showing a relatively downward trend. This appears to reflect slowing growth momentum among tech companies and changes in consumer spending. This contrast between sectors suggests that the market is increasing its preference for stable assets amid uncertainty regarding economic recovery.

This shift in direction can be interpreted as a positive signal related to the pace of global economic recovery. The strength in the energy and healthcare sectors reflects investor sentiment searching for sustainable growth drivers alongside the resumption of economic activity. However, for these trends to persist, additional economic indicators and policy support will likely be necessary. Future market trends are expected to depend heavily on the balance between these sectors and the overall pace of economic recovery.
