## Key Trends This Week
This week's global landscape showed a complex entanglement of subtle shifts in financial markets and escalating geopolitical conflicts. While the policy direction of financial institutions shifted from neutral to a cautiously tightening stance, relations between China and the United States remained tense, raising the possibility of new alliance formations. Changes in technology policy and progress in cryptocurrency regulation also emerged as noteworthy factors. The interplay of these elements is increasing uncertainty regarding the future of the economic landscape.

## [Opacity in Financial Policy] Section
The flow of global financial policy has become increasingly complex from an initially neutral position. **BIS**, the **IMF**, and the **US Federal Reserve (Fed)** maintained a cautious stance initially but have recently shown movements hinting at a shift toward tightening. In particular, despite the stabilization of the **VIX** and **credit spreads**, the weakness of the **US Dollar Index** revealed the complexity of international finance. These changes suggest a reassessment of what roles central banks should play to achieve economic stability. However, as they have since returned to a neutral stance, uncertainty in the financial markets persists.

## [Escalation of Geopolitical Conflict] Section
Relations between China and the United States continued to heighten tensions this week. President Xi Jinping's visits to **Kyrgyzstan** and **Egypt** are interpreted as part of China's efforts to strengthen its geopolitical influence in Central Asia and Africa. This reflects China's strategic movement to seek new alliances amidst conflicts with the US. The activities of the **DOJ** and **FBI** also supported a hardline US foreign policy, indicating an overall trend of escalating geopolitical conflict.

## [Changes in Tech Policy and Cryptocurrency] Section
Technology policy and cryptocurrency regulation are showing positive changes. The **IMF**'s activities related to AI are expected to contribute to promoting global economic activity, with a particular focus on mitigating the negative impacts of conflicts in the Middle East region. Meanwhile, the pursuit of **Bitcoin ETFs** by **BlackRock** and **Goldman Sachs** is sending positive signals to the cryptocurrency market, as financial institutions accelerate their entry into the crypto space.

## [Other Trends]
Developments between **France and the UK** regarding energy geopolitics and issues related to **Operation Gladio** were short-term, yet they suggest that tensions regarding military support still exist. Additionally, the change in the relationship between **JPMorgan** and **Edmond de Rothschild** implies a shift in power structures within financial networks.

## Weekly Conclusion: Looking Ahead to Next Week
This week's trends highlight the impact that financial policy uncertainty and the continuous escalation of geopolitical conflicts have on the global economy. The key for next week will be how these factors interact and evolve. In particular, **changes in the policy direction of central banks**, the **possibility of improved relations between China and the United States**, and **progress in the cryptocurrency regulatory environment** are expected to be major points of interest. These elements are anticipated to have a significant impact on global economic stability and the investment environment.
