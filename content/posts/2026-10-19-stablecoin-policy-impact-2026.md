Recent findings from the Swiss National Bank present a new perspective, suggesting that US interest rate hikes may slow the growth rate of stablecoins. This is particularly noteworthy in the context where an issue that had maintained an 'escalation' stance until four days ago has shifted toward 'de-escalation.' While it was previously expected that interest rate hikes would increase demand for stablecoins, current research presents the possibility of the opposite.

While interest rate hikes increase the returns on traditional financial assets, the value stability and relatively low yields of stablecoins may appear less attractive to investors. This shift reflects a situation where the benefits provided by stablecoins—namely stability and reduced volatility—become relatively less significant compared to the rising yields of traditional assets brought about by interest rate hikes.

These research findings provide important implications for policymakers and investors. Understanding the impact of the Federal Reserve's monetary policy on the digital asset market, particularly on the growth of stablecoins, is essential for establishing future financial strategies. This transition emphasizes that the growth drivers of stablecoins do not rely solely on digital innovation but are heavily dependent on their interaction with the traditional financial environment. Therefore, it is crucial to continuously monitor the interaction between future interest rate trends and the stablecoin market.
