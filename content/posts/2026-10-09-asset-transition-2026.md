The shift in the cryptocurrency industry from a neutral stance to a positive 'bullish' sentiment starting 19 days ago is closely linked to the introduction of CBDCs. According to recent analysis by JPMorgan, the proliferation of Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDC) is facilitating large-scale capital inflows into the cryptocurrency industry while simultaneously preparing for the fragmentation of existing financial systems. This has been shown to reach approximately $5 billion.

This change goes beyond mere technological advancement, suggesting the evolution of the global financial system and the increasing legitimacy and adoption of cryptocurrencies. The introduction of CBDCs serves to blur the boundaries between traditional financial systems and cryptocurrencies, which appears to have led investors to begin perceiving cryptocurrencies as more stable assets.

However, this trend is generating various interpretations within the cryptocurrency industry. Some believe this capital inflow signifies a long-term increase in the value of cryptocurrencies, while others argue it may be the result of short-term market adjustments. The background of these changes appears to involve a complex interplay between global financial innovation and shifts in the regulatory environment.
