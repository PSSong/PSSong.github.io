Experiments with tokenized stocks and government bonds currently being conducted by DTCC and major financial institutions are being evaluated as a significant step toward the future of digital assets. In particular, this issue has shown a shift in direction from a bullish stance to an escalation starting eight days ago, suggesting the possibility of changes in digital currency policy.

This transition goes beyond mere technical experimentation, indicating that discussions regarding the introduction of Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDC) are entering an increasingly urgent and practical stage. While tokenized asset experiments are a process of verifying the efficiency and stability of digital assets within the financial system, they simultaneously reflect movements by central banks to redefine their roles in a digital currency environment.

These changes demand both innovation in the financial system and the evolution of the regulatory environment. Depending on the progress and results of these experiments, discussions regarding the introduction of CBDCs are likely to accelerate further. However, at the same time, caution is required regarding the technical, legal, and social challenges that may arise during this process. Financial markets and policymakers must carefully observe these trends of change and readjust their strategies for future monetary policy.
