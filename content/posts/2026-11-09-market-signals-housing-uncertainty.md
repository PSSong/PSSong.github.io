The changes in the housing market that have continued since 21 days ago show that the difficulty of purchasing homes persists even in a low-interest rate environment, which is having a significant impact on investor sentiment. Initially, low-interest rate policies were expected to have a positive effect on the housing market, but recent trends have reversed from 'bullish' to 'bearish.' This shift reflects the complexity and structural issues of the housing market and clearly reveals the psychological changes in investors.

Real estate expert Ryan Serhant's remarks emphasize that rising home prices and strict lending conditions make home buying difficult even in a low-interest rate environment. This situation creates anxiety for homebuyers and investors, negatively impacting the investment decision-making of market participants. The reasons why low-interest rate policies are not as effective as expected stem from various factors: high home prices, complexity of loan conditions, and economic instability are all working in combination.

These market signals provide important clues for predicting future trends in the housing market. Through these changes, investors must recognize that uncertainty in the housing market is increasing and understand that a more cautious approach is required. Therefore, the phenomenon of persistent difficulties in the housing market despite low interest rates is driving investor sentiment to become even more conservative, which is expected to act as a significant variable in long-term market trends.
