As investor sentiment shifts from 'bearish' to a neutral stance, patterns of asset rotation are changing. While recession concerns dominated the market until the 25th, investment strategies now prominently seek both stability and future growth potential. The strength in consumer staples and utilities sectors demonstrates that investors are regaining confidence in defensive stocks. Conversely, the weakness in the technology sector reflects a preference for market stability alongside uncertainty regarding growth stocks.

These changes indicate a movement toward constructing more balanced portfolios, despite the continued existence of economic uncertainty. The strength of defensive stocks can be interpreted as part of a strategy that simultaneously explores short-term stability and long-term growth potential. In predicting future market trends, these sector-specific flows will serve as important indicators for shifts in investor sentiment.
