Several complex factors are driving this transition. Key drivers include signs of a global economic recovery, improving corporate earnings, and changes in central bank monetary policies. In particular, the fact that recent central bank interest rate policies are being adjusted toward supporting economic growth is sending positive signals to investors. Along with the realignment of interests within the financial power structure, these changes reflect an optimistic outlook for the future economic environment.

Market volatility and uncertainty still exist, and investors must keep in mind that geopolitical issues or unexpected economic shocks could shift market trends once again. Therefore, while JP Morgan's forecast is a positive signal, it is crucial to closely analyze the complex market dynamics hidden behind it.
