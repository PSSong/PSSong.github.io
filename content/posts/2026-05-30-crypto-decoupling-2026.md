Over the past five days, the cryptocurrency market has been moving in a direction different from macroeconomic trends. The shift from the previous bullish trend to a bearish one reflects the short-term weakness of BTC. This suggests that despite the increase in investment in risky assets in the global market, the cryptocurrency market is undergoing a selective adjustment in capital flows. Investors appear to be considering various factors, such as increased regulation on cryptocurrencies, technical factors, or the reallocation of funds to other asset classes. This decoupling phenomenon highlights the independence of the cryptocurrency market and provides important implications for future investment strategies.
