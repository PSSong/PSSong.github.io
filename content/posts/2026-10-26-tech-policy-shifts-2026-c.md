Recent moves by JPMorgan and Citi reflect a reassessment of the role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology within the financial industry. Previously, these two companies emphasized the autonomy of AI agents amidst a bullish trend driving technological innovation; however, they are now shifting toward a neutral stance, re-examining the importance of human intervention. This change can be interpreted as part of an effort to ensure stability and transparency in financial markets.

Behind this transition lie the ethical and legal issues arising alongside the rapid advancement of AI technology. In particular, as concerns grow regarding risk management related to opaque AI decision-making processes, companies are moving toward strengthening human oversight alongside technological progress. This approach focuses on more than just regulatory compliance; it aims to maintain customer trust and ensure market stability.

Industry experts predict that these changes could have a positive impact on the future of financial technology. By maximizing AI's capabilities while combining them with human judgment and ethical sense, it appears possible to effectively manage the complexities of the financial system. Therefore, the decisions made by JPMorgan and Citi can be interpreted as more than just a change in corporate policy; they represent a significant turning point for the global financial industry as it seeks a balance between technology and human intervention.
