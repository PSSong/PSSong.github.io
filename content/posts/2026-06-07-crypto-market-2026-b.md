The continuous decline in BTC and gold prices reflects recent changes in the economic environment, with inflation concerns and the strength of the dollar emerging as key factors. This trend, which has persisted for nine days, is being interpreted as a significant signal shifting market sentiment from neutral to bearish.

While gold and BTC have traditionally been considered hedges against inflation, the recent downward trend raises questions about this role. This suggests that inflationary pressures in the global economy are still persisting, prompting investors to accelerate their movement toward safe-haven assets. In particular, the strength of the dollar is impacting international capital flows and playing a role in reducing demand for other asset classes.

These changes are amplifying concerns regarding domestic inflation while simultaneously prompting investors to consider reallocating to more stable assets. The decline in BTC and gold prices can be interpreted as more than just price volatility; it serves as an indicator reflecting economic uncertainty and the impact of monetary policy. Future market trends are expected to depend heavily on inflation management and the stability of currency values.
