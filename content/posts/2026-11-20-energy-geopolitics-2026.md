Recent dollar strength and falling gold prices suggest shifts in geopolitical stability, indicating that tensions from four days ago are easing. The transition from an 'escalation' stance to 'de-escalation' implies that concerns regarding the global economy and political situation are somewhat being resolved. The strengthening of the dollar reinforces its role as a safe-haven asset, while the decline in gold prices reflects a mitigation in market perception of economic instability.

These changes can be interpreted as signals that international political tensions are easing and global economic stability is recovering. However, at the same time, if this trend continues, the possibility of excessive reliance on the dollar and the potential revaluation of other safe-haven assets, such as gold, must also be considered. The recovery of geopolitical stability is expected to have significant impacts on economic policies and investment strategies. Therefore, future trends in the dollar and gold prices will serve as crucial indicators for continuously monitoring changes in global economic stability and the geopolitical environment.
