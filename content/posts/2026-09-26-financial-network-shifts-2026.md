Up until 18 days ago, the strategies of BlackRock and JPMorgan were proceeding under a positive market outlook ('bullish'), but have recently shifted toward a rapid 'escalation.' This change signifies an acceleration in the efforts of these two financial giants to enhance the security and efficiency of the financial system through digital tokenization strategies and blockchain technology.

BlackRock's digital asset strategy is expanding investment accessibility by converting traditional financial assets into digital forms. Conversely, JPMorgan is strengthening the transparency and security of transactions through blockchain-based solutions, seeking to expand international capital control structures. These movements suggest a fundamental reconfiguration of the global financial network beyond mere technological advancement.

Behind this change lies technological innovation alongside shifts in the financial regulatory environment. Along with technical support from agencies such as DARPA, this reflects the will of financial institutions to maintain sustainable influence in the future financial ecosystem. This transition is accelerating structural changes in financial markets and presenting a new paradigm for the flow and management of international capital.
