On October 15, 2026, recent announcements regarding the Fed's interest rate policy are causing confusion among market participants. Over the past month, the Fed has shifted from a neutral stance toward a more "hawkish" direction without providing clear signals during this process. This ambiguous approach has resulted in amplified market uncertainty.

This shift by the Fed reflects concerns over economic indicator volatility and inflation management. However, with a specific policy direction remaining unclear, investors are opting for risk aversion amidst the uncertainty. This is manifesting as an expansion of HY credit spreads and a rise in the VIX index. In particular, the increase in HY credit spreads suggests a heightened perception of credit risk within the corporate bond market, while the rise in the VIX index directly reflects investor anxiety in the market.

While these ambiguous signals from the Fed reveal the complexity of the policymaking process, they also make it difficult for market participants to predict future economic conditions. Consequently, market speculation and analysis aimed at predicting the Fed's next move are expected to intensify. This is highly likely to further increase market volatility.

In this environment, investors are awaiting clear signals regarding the Fed's future policy direction while simultaneously readjusting their risk management strategies. Interest is mounting as to how the Fed will resolve uncertainty and secure market stability in the future.
