The loan received by El Salvador from the IMF is emerging as a significant case demonstrating the complex interactions among various actors within the international financial network. While tensions escalated during the initial loan negotiation process, the situation has recently stabilized to a 'neutral' state, which can be interpreted as the result of adjustments and compromises between both parties.

This loan goes beyond mere economic support; it reflects the relationship between El Salvador's cryptocurrency policy and international financial institutions. In particular, since El Salvador adopted Bitcoin as legal tender, its relationship with the IMF has become increasingly complex. While the IMF has expressed concerns regarding cryptocurrency from the perspective of traditional financial stability and regulation, this loan suggests the possibility for both parties to cooperate based on mutual understanding.

The neutralization of the loan demonstrates that El Salvador is seeking a balance between innovation through cryptocurrency and traditional financial stability within the international financial system. This serves as an important case study showing how cryptocurrency policies are being accommodated within international financial networks, providing crucial clues for gauging the future direction of cryptocurrency policy and international financial cooperation.

Therefore, El Salvador's recent loan is expected to serve as a catalyst for discussions regarding the role and future of cryptocurrency within the global financial system, moving beyond simple economic assistance.
