As price volatility in the recent crypto asset market has noticeably increased, expectations regarding the implementation of Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDC) appear to be being reflected in the market. This phenomenon reflects investors' interest in the flexibility of digital currencies and new financial systems. However, critics point out that more macroeconomic indicators and policy clarity are required to establish a trend for this volatility.

This issue, which has persisted over the past 19 days, initially showed a positive outlook—a 'bullish' sentiment—for the crypto asset market. Recently, however, this atmosphere has shifted to 'neutral.' This change is interpreted as a reflection of policy uncertainty regarding CBDC implementation, alongside an awareness of the complexities within the global economic landscape. In particular, the current lack of clear guidelines regarding the speed and direction of CBDC adoption by central banks is amplifying market instability.

This transition demands a cautious approach from crypto asset investors and once again underscores the importance of transparent communication for policymakers. Moving forward, specific policy announcements related to CBDC implementation and changes in economic indicators are expected to further clarify the market's direction.
