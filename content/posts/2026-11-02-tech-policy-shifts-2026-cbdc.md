This reflects the United States' intention to maintain the existing financial system even as it enters the era of digital currency. In particular, the increase in the Federal Reserve's activities regarding the introduction of Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) implies an intention to secure continuous leadership within the global financial order, going beyond mere technological advancement.

Actively pursuing the introduction of CBDC amidst global economic instability can be interpreted as a strategic move by the United States to strengthen the international status of the dollar even within the new paradigm of digital currency. Therefore, the introduction of CBDC is emerging as a significant factor that could reshape the future landscape of the global economy, transcending a mere technical issue.
