As Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi has recently publicly emphasized strengthening cooperation with Russian President Vladimir Putin, interest in the geopolitical alliance structure of the Indo-Pacific region is growing. This move by Prime Minister Modi can be interpreted as more than a simple diplomatic gesture; it serves as a signal that India is seeking new alliance relationships within global political and economic currents.

In particular, amidst the ongoing impact of the Ukraine war, Prime Minister Modi's remarks appear to demonstrate India's intention to strengthen its relationship with Russia. This may be part of India's effort to balance its traditional alliance with the United States and its relationship with Russia. However, this movement has the potential to cause complex changes in alliances when coupled with the reactions of other countries within the Indo-Pacific region.

These cooperative moves are particularly noteworthy in the context of Indo-Pacific strategy. While India has sought to maintain strategic neutrality amidst the competition between the United States and China, recent trends suggest an intention by India to expand its influence within the region through cooperation with Russia. This could serve as a catalyst for readjusting the geopolitical alliance structures of East and South Asia.

However, these changes are expected to go beyond mere improvement in relations between the two countries and influence the strategic judgments of neighboring nations. For example, South Korea and Japan will likely be closely monitoring how the strengthening of cooperation between India and Russia will bring changes to the East Asian security environment. This illustrates the intersection of complex interests related to the formation of new alliance patterns within the Indo-Pacific region.

In conclusion, the cooperative moves between Modi and Putin possess the potential to reshape the geopolitical landscape of the Indo-Pacific region, moving beyond simple diplomatic relations between the two nations. The flow of these changes is expected to become even more apparent in future international conferences and multilateral cooperation.
