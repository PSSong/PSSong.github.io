## Key Flows This Week
Global markets showed complex signals this week amid financial policy uncertainty and geopolitical tensions. As the stance of financial institutions gradually adjusted toward a neutral position, the ambiguity of monetary policy strengthened market risk aversion. On the other hand, despite ongoing geopolitical tensions, positive movements such as peace agreements in certain regions were detected, and technology and asset rotation trends are presenting new opportunities to investors. The complex interplay of these factors has created a situation where predicting market direction is difficult.

## [Monetary Policy and Market Uncertainty]
**Ambiguity in Monetary Policy and Market Instability**
This week, the direction of monetary policy emerged as a major concern amid global economic uncertainty. Initially, a hawkish stance from the Fed was observed, but it later transitioned to a neutral position. Along with the rise in the DXY (Dollar Index), the widening of HY credit spreads reflected the market's risk-averse tendency, and VIX volatility remained at a high level. Nevertheless, some narrowing of credit spreads and the stabilization of the VIX further increased uncertainty regarding market direction. These changes in financial institutions suggest that investors are taking a cautious approach amidst overall economic uncertainty.

## [Geopolitical Tensions and Escalations]
**Geopolitical Tensions and Ongoing Escalation**
Geopolitical issues showed continuous escalation. In particular, the geopolitical situation became more complex as the roles of financial institutions such as JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs were highlighted alongside issues related to the Trump administration. Although there were positive movements in some regions, such as the peace agreement between Iran and the UAE, the overall trend remains tense. This situation continues to impact the international economy and financial markets.

## [Investment and Market Trends]
**Changes in Investment Strategies and Positive Signals**
Changes in the investment strategies of major financial institutions, such as Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan, are injecting new momentum into the market. Goldman Sachs' upward revision of its gold price forecast and BlackRock's proposal for innovative retirement strategies provide positive signals to investors. Furthermore, despite inflation concerns related to rising wages in the Korean semiconductor industry, the sector rotation trend among market participants indicates an optimistic outlook for certain assets.

## [Other Trends]
- **Tech Sector and SpaceX**: The tech sector maintains a positive trend driven by the increase in SpaceX's valuation, but volatility in BTC and the VIX still reflects market uncertainty.
- **Energy Geopolitics**: Tensions regarding energy security between Iran and the United States continue.

## Weekly Conclusion: A Perspective for Next Week
The complex flows of this week are expected to continue into next week. While the ambiguity of monetary policy and geopolitical tensions are expected to sustain market instability, positive changes in investment strategies and the upward trend of certain assets may provide new opportunities for investors. In particular, structural changes in central banks and progress in technology regulations are observed to be significant variables for future market trends. The market's direction will likely be determined by how these factors interact.
