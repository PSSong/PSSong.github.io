May 24, 2026. The rapid advancement of AI technology is emerging as a new tool for diversifying geopolitical risks. While concerns over geopolitical instability and slowing economic growth have intensified globally in recent weeks, an optimistic narrative regarding the potential and growth possibilities of AI technology is rapidly spreading as a response.

Until four days ago, a trend of "escalation"—characterized by rising geopolitical tensions and economic uncertainty—was dominant. However, through a recent shift in direction, technological optimism is being presented as a solution to mitigate geopolitical risks. This shift carries significance beyond merely reflecting the natural flow of technological progress. Governments and corporations are spreading the message that they can manage future uncertainties and reignite economic growth through innovation in AI technology.

The dual purpose of this narrative can be summarized in two points. First, it aims to temporarily mask immediate geopolitical instability through the advancement of AI technology. Second, it presents an optimistic outlook of finding new drivers for economic growth through technological innovation in the long term. This approach serves to shift public attention toward the positive aspects of technological progress while simultaneously playing a role in alleviating political and economic pressures.

However, such an optimistic narrative also requires caution. The actual application and effectiveness of AI technology are still in the verification stage, and technical challenges and ethical issues must be considered alongside them. Therefore, when evaluating the potential of AI technology as a tool to diversify geopolitical risks, the underlying complexities and potential risks must also be taken into account.

This shift goes beyond the mere rise of technological optimism; it reflects the will of global leadership to present future-oriented solutions amidst geopolitical uncertainty. However, readers need to think deeply about the limitations and challenges of technological progress alongside the positive outlooks provided by this narrative.
