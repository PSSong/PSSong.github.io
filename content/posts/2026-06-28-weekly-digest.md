## Key Trends This Week
The global situation this week showed a pattern of continuously escalating geopolitical tensions amidst relative stability in financial markets and changes in technology and asset markets. While financial markets maintained a neutral state without significant volatility in major indicators, various signals regarding technology investment and asset reallocation emerged. In particular, the movements of AI and technology companies, alongside warnings from Goldman Sachs and BlackRock, complicated the market's direction. Meanwhile, on the geopolitical front, relations between the United States, China, and Iran continued to remain tense.

## [Financial Market Stability]
**Key Tags: BIS, Fed, VIX, dollar-index**

This week, the financial market maintained a neutral state without significant volatility in major indicators. The movements of the (BIS) and (Fed) did not cause major shocks to the market, and credit spreads and the dollar index also showed a tendency toward stability. This stability suggests that global financial institutions are cautiously monitoring the current economic environment. However, even amidst this stability, market participants are maintaining a stance of preparing for potential uncertainties.

## [Technology Investment and Asset Reallocation]
**Key Tags: AI-investment, SK-Hynix, Wall-Street-IPOs**

Technology investment and asset reallocation were among the major trends this week. Goldman Sachs cautioned against excessive optimism and advised re-evaluating the actual performance and limitations of AI investments. On the other hand, SK Hynix's US listing sent a positive signal for asset reallocation, and the revival of the Wall Street IPO market is expected to have a positive impact on the investment environment. These trends demonstrate that investment flows in the technology sector continue to shift.

**Key Tags: US-China, US-Imran, geopolitical-interests**
