The cryptocurrency industry is reaching a new turning point due to recent recommendations from the Regulatory Rationalization Committee, which operates directly under the President. These recommendations propose an easing of the operating environment for companies that have faced difficulties due to strict regulations, drawing significant interest and expectation within the industry.

Over the past few weeks, the virtual asset industry has struggled amidst a wave of rapid regulatory tightening. However, these recommendations are serving as an opportunity to reverse this trend. In particular, this shift in direction signifies a transition from 'escalation' to 'de-escalation,' suggesting the possibility of stable growth for virtual assets alongside strengthened communication between the government and the industry. 

Beyond mere deregulation, these recommendations reflect a commitment to creating an environment where the virtual asset industry can achieve global competitiveness and accelerate innovation. However, how these changes will actually impact the industry remains to be seen, as it will depend on the government's specific future implementation plans and market reactions. Therefore, the industry appears to need to use these recommendations as an opportunity to establish continuous monitoring and adaptation strategies for future policy changes.
