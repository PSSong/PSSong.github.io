While there were initial concerns that the merger would cause job instability, the situation is showing a trend toward stabilization as recent developments have shifted from 'de-escalation' to 'neutral.'

This shift suggests that the merger does not simply imply job losses but also holds the potential to create new opportunities through the introduction of new business models and technologies. Particularly in regions such as California and Nashville, employment in digital media and content production is expected to increase as existing employment structures are reorganized. This transition reflects lessons learned from past merger cases, moving toward minimizing economic instability and pursuing qualitative changes in employment.

Ultimately, beyond the short-term shock to the job market, this merger can be interpreted as a signal that promotes the redistribution of capabilities and innovation within the industry in the long term. This will be recorded as an example showing how companies create new opportunities while adapting to a changing market environment.
