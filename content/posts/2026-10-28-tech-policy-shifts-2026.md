The process of Anthropic's IPO has emerged as a significant case demonstrating the close linkage between technology companies and traditional financial institutions. As the core roles of Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are emphasized, the cooperation between these entities has shifted from 'neutral' to 'escalation.' This change suggests that the convergence of technological innovation and financial services is accelerating.

Behind this transition lies the intention of tech companies to more actively utilize the resources of traditional financial markets for growth and fundraising. Particularly, alongside the rapid advancement of AI technology, as these companies occupy an increasingly important position in the global financial market, the role of traditional financial institutions is being further emphasized.

However, this strengthening linkage is simultaneously triggering discussions regarding regulation and ethical issues. It is crucial to balance the evaluation of potential risks and opportunities brought by the combination of technological innovation and financial services. Future trends will likely depend on how the cooperation model between tech companies and financial institutions evolves.
