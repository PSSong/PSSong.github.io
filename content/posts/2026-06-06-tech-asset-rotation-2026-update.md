In June 2026, the technology and healthcare sectors are showing strength in the market, while the energy and consumer discretionary sectors are recording weakness, clearly demonstrating a shift in market risk appetite. This issue has become even clearer through the transition from an 'escalation' stance up until 15 days ago to a 'bullish' one. This transition reflects the tendency of investors to seek stable growth potential as initial tensions and anxieties gradually dissipate.

The strength in the technology sector reflects the continuous demand for digital transformation and innovative technologies, while the rise in the healthcare sector is attributed to population aging and increasing interest in healthcare. Conversely, the weakness in the energy and consumer discretionary sectors suggests uncertainty in economic growth and weakening consumer sentiment. This rotation between sectors is a natural phenomenon that occurs as market participants search for future growth drivers.

These trends suggest that the market is overall attempting to transition from risk aversion to stable growth. However, whether this change will persist will largely depend on various variables, such as the direction of central bank policies, global economic indicators, and geopolitical factors. Moving forward, the market should focus on how these imbalances between sectors will be adjusted.
