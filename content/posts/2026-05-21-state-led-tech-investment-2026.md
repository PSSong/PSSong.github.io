Observations are being made that the competition for technological hegemony could move beyond a simple confrontational structure and lead to opportunities for cooperation for mutual benefit. This shift can be read as an intention to strengthen U.S. leadership in the global technological innovation ecosystem while simultaneously creating greater momentum for innovation through cooperation with the international community.

In conclusion, the recent shift in direction presents a positive outlook for technological advancement and is noteworthy in that the competition for technological hegemony is moving beyond mere rivalry to open up the possibility of mutual cooperation.
