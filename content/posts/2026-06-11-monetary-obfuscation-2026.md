On June 11, 2026, despite the Fed's decision to freeze interest rates, financial markets remain in a state of high uncertainty. This suggests that the absence of clear signals regarding the Fed's policy direction is causing confusion among market participants. Recent Fed movements have shown a pattern of shifting from a neutral stance toward a more hawkish direction. While this change may be a result of considering economic uncertainty and inflationary pressures, it has simultaneously amplified unpredictability in the market.

The rise in HY credit spreads and the VIX index shows that investors are increasing their perception of economic risk. On the other hand, the strength of Bitcoin (BTC) and certain growth sectors suggests that risk-on elements still exist. This means that the Fed's policy ambiguity is triggering ambivalent reactions within the market. As long as the Fed does not clarify its future policy direction, this uncertainty is expected to continue influencing market trends.

Market participants are closely watching the Fed's next move, continuously readjusting their interpretations of how policy decisions will impact the overall economy. Ultimately, the Fed's ambiguous signals are prompting investors to make more cautious judgments and prepare for various scenarios.
