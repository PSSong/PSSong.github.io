By shifting its outlook on hyperscaler investments from neutral to positive, JPMorgan is once again emphasizing the close relationship between changes in technology policy and capital flows. This transition moves away from the neutral stance held just 17 days ago, reflecting a new optimism regarding future investment trends in technological infrastructure.

This change is occurring alongside improvements in the global technology regulatory environment. With the easing of tech policies, hyperscaler companies are now able to focus more on developing innovative solutions for strengthening data security and privacy. These policy shifts are positively impacting companies' decisions to expand investments in cloud services and data center expansion.

Furthermore, as social demand for building sustainable technological infrastructure increases, hyperscalers are also intensifying investments in green technologies and the construction of energy-efficient data centers. This trend demonstrates that tech companies are not merely pursuing growth but are taking a strategic approach toward a sustainable future.

However, despite this positive outlook, uncertainties in technology policy and changes in the global regulatory environment can still act as investment risk factors. Investors will need to adjust their hyperscaler investment strategies while carefully monitoring the progress of policy changes and technological innovation.
