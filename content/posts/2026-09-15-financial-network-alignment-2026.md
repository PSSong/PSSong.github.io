September 15, 2026 - As major global banks, including Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan, have recently announced unified outlooks on monetary policy, organic communication and cooperation within international financial networks are being further emphasized. Previously, differences in positions among these banks amplified uncertainty in financial markets, but the recent alignment of views is expected to have a positive impact on market stability and predictability.

Notably, this issue is characterized by a shift from an 'escalation' phase to a 'neutral' stance seven days ago. This transition reflects a reduction in tension within the global financial system, alongside a stronger commitment to cooperation among international financial institutions. It is highly likely that the strengthening of financial regulation and cooperation, centered around the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), serves as the backdrop for this change. The movement of banks toward common goals can be interpreted as a signal of enhanced financial system stability and cooperation for global economic recovery.

Nevertheless, the impact of such unified views on the market is complex. On one hand, investor confidence may increase due to reduced uncertainty; on the other hand, concerns are being raised that excessive alignment could limit diversity and creativity. Therefore, financial market participants will need to closely observe these trends and prepare for various scenarios.
