October 20, 2026—The rapid growth of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology is driving up copper prices, reflecting a new paradigm in the digital economy. Until just three days ago, the rising copper prices due to increased AI demand were characterized by an 'escalation' trend that could cause market instability; however, they are now showing a positive transition. Activities related to Bitcoin ETFs by JPMorgan and BlackRock support this shift.

Copper is an essential raw material for building AI infrastructure, and demand is surging alongside the increase in data centers and high-performance computing devices. This can be interpreted as more than just a rise in commodity prices; it serves as an indicator of the impact technological innovation has on economic structures.

Meanwhile, the movements related to Bitcoin ETFs by JPMorgan and BlackRock are being interpreted as positive signals for the transition to digital currency. These activities suggest that traditional financial institutions are increasing their trust in the cryptocurrency market, which may accelerate progress in digital currency policies such as CBDCs (Central Bank Digital Currencies).

The background of this transition involves a complex interplay between technological innovation and the evolution of financial markets. While the growth of AI technology has driven up copper prices, the participation of financial institutions in the cryptocurrency market demonstrates a positive trend toward the transition to digital currency. It is time to closely observe the impact these changes will have on the future global economy and financial systems.
