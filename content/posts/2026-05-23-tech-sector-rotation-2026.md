Over the past five days, a noticeable change has been captured in the financial markets: capital is once again concentrating in the technology and semiconductor sectors. This can be interpreted as reflecting a shift in recent market sentiment from 'escalation' to 'bullish'. As previous tensions and uncertainties gradually dissipate, investors appear to be once again searching for future growth drivers.

In particular, the rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology and its subsequent industrial ripple effects are acting as key factors accelerating this capital flow. AI-related companies are attracting investor interest based on their innovative technologies and high growth potential. However, this does not simply signify investment in growth drivers alone.

At the same time, the tech sector is being recognized as a safe haven amidst recent credit crises and economic uncertainty. Investors tend to move funds into stable and robust tech companies whenever market volatility increases. This suggests that the tech sector serves not only as an investment destination for future growth but also as a means of risk aversion.

This shift in capital flow is a prime example of the complexity of the global economy. On one hand, there are optimistic outlooks for innovation and growth, while on the other, there is a demand for stable investment destinations amidst uncertainty. Investors are adjusting their portfolios in consideration of this duality, and future market trends will largely depend on how this flow evolves.

Market experts are observing that if this rotation of capital continues, the strength of the tech sector could persist for a longer period. However, they also provide the advice that careful monitoring is necessary to see how these sectors will react if market instability rises again.
