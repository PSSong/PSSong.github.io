Large-scale net outflows from Bitcoin ETFs are persisting, suggesting that investors are further strengthening their risk aversion. This phenomenon began to be observed 20 days ago; while the market initially showed a 'de-escalation' trend, it has recently shifted toward a 'bearish' direction.

This shift clearly demonstrates that demand for alternative assets such as Bitcoin is decreasing. Investors are showing a tendency to move funds into safer assets, which is closely linked to the strengthening of central bank tightening policies alongside global economic uncertainty.

Net outflows from Bitcoin ETFs reflect a change in broader market sentiment beyond just issues within the cryptocurrency market. Investors turning toward traditional safe-haven assets signifies increasing anxiety regarding the economic outlook. This trend can serve as an important indicator for predicting future trends in not only the cryptocurrency market but also the overall financial markets.

Therefore, net outflows from Bitcoin ETFs should be interpreted as a signal that reinforces investors' psychological shifts and the overall market risk aversion, moving beyond simple flows between asset types. These trends provide important insights into how investors may need to adjust their approach to various assets in the future.
